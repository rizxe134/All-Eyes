Camera object detection uses SSDLite MobileNet v2 from TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD.
The model code and weights are Copyright Google LLC, Apache License 2.0.
A copy of that license ships at models/ssdlite/LICENSE.

Earth day and night images are NASA Blue Marble and city lights, public domain,
as distributed with the three-globe example assets.
The color theme shows them in natural color, with city lights on the night side.
The green theme grades the same images to phosphor at runtime.
